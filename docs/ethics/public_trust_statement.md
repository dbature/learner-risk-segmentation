# Public trust statement

**What this is.** A tool that helps learning coaches decide whom to contact
first. Thirty days into a course, it looks at signs such as whether early
assignments were handed in and how often a learner has been active online,
and it gives each learner a risk score with the reasons behind it.

**What it is not.** It does not decide anything. A coach decides, and can
always disagree with it. It never contacts a learner, never affects marks or
places, and is never shown to learners as a label.

**What it does not use.** Names, sex, age, disability or where someone
lives. We keep some of these aside only to check that the tool finds
learners who need help equally well across groups.

**How well it works, honestly.** If coaches can see a quarter of a cohort,
the tool points them to about 45 in every 100 learners who would otherwise
not complete, nearly twice as many as choosing at random. It misses the
rest, often because the reasons leave no trace online.

**Where it falls short.** Our fairness checks found that it reaches learners
from the most deprived areas more often than those from the least deprived,
and that the gap for disabled learners is just over our limit. Until an
independent committee rules on these results, the tool is not in use with
real learners.

**How we keep it in check.** Every prediction is recorded with its reasons.
Performance and fairness are re-checked for every new group of learners on
a public monitoring page. An ethics committee, including an independent
member, can stop the tool at any time.

**Contact.** Questions or objections go to the programme's Data Protection
Officer. Code, model card and fairness report:
https://github.com/dbature/learner-risk-segmentation
